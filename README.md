## 🧠 CognitiveOps — What I Stand For

<b>CognitiveOps</b> is the evolution of MLOps.  
Not just deploying models — but operating **intelligence itself**.

I focus on:
- 🧩 Cognitive pipelines instead of static ML workflows  
- 🔁 Systems that **reason, remember, and improve over time**
- ⚙️ AI as an **operational layer**, not a feature
- 🧠 Bridging LLMs, ML, infra, and human feedback into one loop

My goal is to engineer **AI systems that behave like thinking entities**, not scripts.
